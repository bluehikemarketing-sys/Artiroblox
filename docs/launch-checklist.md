# Clean by Morning launch checklist

One list for everything, in order. Details and sources are in `docs/before-the-first-job.md` and `docs/hiring-and-quality-control.md`.

## Before your first walkthrough

**Business and legal**
- [ ] Florida LLC active on sunbiz.org (annual report paid)
- [ ] NY Application for Authority filed (~$250)
- [ ] "Clean by Morning" filed as an assumed name ($25). Send Claude the exact LLC name so the website shows the right legal name
- [ ] EIN and business bank account
- [ ] NY sales tax Certificate of Authority applied for (needs 20 days before your first job)
- [ ] Workers' comp exemption certificate CE-200 (free)

**Insurance**
- [ ] General liability $1M/$2M bound, certificate of insurance (COI) saved as a PDF
- [ ] Janitorial bond
- [ ] Auto insurer told you drive to jobs with equipment

**Business credit**
- [ ] D-U-N-S number (free)
- [ ] One business address (virtual mailbox) and the 516 number, identical everywhere
- [ ] 2 net-30 supplier accounts (Uline, Quill or Grainger), paid early

**Online presence**
- [x] Website live
- [x] jamel@cleanbymorning.com set up
- [ ] Gmail "Send mail as" jamel@ working
- [x] Google Business Profile submitted (verification pending)
- [ ] Google Search Console verified, sitemap submitted
- [ ] Bing Places and Apple Business Connect
- [ ] LinkedIn company page (send Claude the profile links)
- [ ] Formspree link for the walkthrough form (send Claude the link)
- [ ] Your photo and 2–3 sentence bio for the About page (send to Claude)

**Cold email**
- [ ] 3 sending domains bought, each forwarding to cleanbymorning.com
- [ ] 6 inboxes with SPF, DKIM and DMARC records, warming or pre-warmed
- [ ] Sending platform set up
- [ ] Lead list of 300–500 medical and dental practices (Garden City, Mineola, Great Neck first)
- [ ] Sequences loaded (`docs/sales/email-sequences.md`)
- [ ] Every email has your postal address and an unsubscribe link

**Walkthrough kit**
- [ ] Walkthrough checklist (`docs/sales/walkthrough-and-closing.md`)
- [ ] Your internal rate sheet (not on the website)
- [ ] Proposal template
- [ ] Capability statement printed (cleanbymorning.com/one-pager/)
- [ ] COI and CE-200 PDFs on your phone

## Before your first job

- [ ] Sales tax certificate received
- [ ] Service agreement signed (have an attorney review the template once)
- [ ] First month paid, or a 50% deposit on a project
- [ ] Site checklist written, room by room
- [ ] Key and alarm-code log started
- [ ] Building vendor registration done and COI approved
- [ ] Invoice template with sales tax on its own line (8.875% NYC / 8.625% Nassau)
- [ ] Supplies:
  - [ ] Color-coded microfiber cloths
  - [ ] EPA-registered disinfectant sold in NY
  - [ ] Mop system
  - [ ] Vacuum
  - [ ] Trash liners
  - [ ] Gloves
  - [ ] Safety data sheet (SDS) binder

## Within 120 days of the NY filing

- [ ] Newspaper notice published (6 weeks, 2 papers)
- [ ] Certificate of Publication filed ($50)

## Before your first hire

- [ ] Workers' comp, disability and paid family leave policies in place
- [ ] Payroll set up (W-2, never 1099)
- [ ] I-9 and written pay notice
- [ ] House rules and electronic monitoring notice signed
- [ ] Paid trial shift done, background check run only after a written offer

## Before your first project job (post-construction, turnovers)

- [ ] COI lists the contractor as additional insured, with a waiver of subrogation and primary and non-contributory wording
- [ ] Ask the contractor in writing whether it's a Site Safety Plan site (if so, SST cards are needed)
- [ ] Equipment rental lined up (HEPA vacuum, ladder, floor machine)
- [ ] 50% deposit received
- [ ] Never: mold over 10 sq ft, hauling debris, pest control, biohazard
