# Which kind of commercial cleaning to start with

Commercial cleaning covers many kinds of work. Some pay more but can't be won yet by a new company that sells by cold email. This sorts them by what's logical now, next, and later.

## The five filters

| Filter | The question | Why it matters for you |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reachable by email | Does one person decide, and can you find their email? | Cold email works on owners and office managers. It fails against purchasing departments and formal bids |
| 2. Winnable as a newcomer | Can you win with insurance and a good walkthrough, without years of references, bonding or certifications? | Many high-paying segments screen out new companies |
| 3. Doable solo | Can one person clean it in a few hours a night? | You're the crew until about Month 4 |
| 4. Contract size | At least ~$500 a month, or a one-off worth $500+? | Small contracts aren't worth the drive |
| 5. Startup cost and risk | Special equipment, licenses, liability? | $1,000 doesn't buy ride-on scrubbers |

## Every segment, scored (5 = best for you right now)

Monthly values are rough NY-area estimates, not sourced figures.

| Segment | Email reach | Winnable new | Solo | Contract size | Startup cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Apartment turnovers (one-offs) | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 (~$200–450/unit, repeat flow) | 5 | **Start now, for cash.** Daytime work |

| Car dealerships | 3 | 2 | 2 | 5 ($2k+/mo) | 3 | **Months 6–12.** Needs a crew; several people decide |

## What unlocks each tier

| When you have… | You can go after… |
|---|---|
| $1M/$2M liability insurance + the CE-200 workers' comp exemption | Medical, dental, small offices, gyms |
| 3–5 happy clients, reviews and photos | Bigger practices, property managers, daycares |
| Contractor-level insurance paperwork (additional insured, waiver of subrogation) | Post-construction for contractors |
| First crew + a van | 5–7-night gyms, multi-site clients, dealerships |
| Floor machines + training | Floor care add-ons, warehouses |
| Medical references + infection-control training | Surgery centers, urgent care |
| Bonding + 2–3 years of history + certified payroll | Schools, government |
| A decision on unions + a large crew | Large Manhattan office buildings |

## Let the data pick the winner

1. **Weeks 1–3:** send 3 test campaigns of ~300 contacts each: medical/dental, small professional offices, gyms and studios.
2. **Track for each:** positive reply rate, walkthroughs booked, close rate, monthly contract value, hours to clean, drive time.
3. **Week 4:** move ~70% of sending to the segment with the most **monthly revenue per 1,000 emails**. Keep ~30% testing a new segment each month.
4. **Always:** a small campaign to contractors and property managers for project jobs, the cash that funds the rest.

## Recommendation

Lead with medical and dental, add small professional offices for quick references, test gyms and studios, and do apartment turnovers and post-construction in the daytime for cash. Everything else waits for its unlock.
