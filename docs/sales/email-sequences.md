# Cold email sequences

Four target groups, three emails each: day 1, day 4 and day 9. Send from a separate warmed-up domain (for example `getcleanbymorning.com`), never from `cleanbymorning.com`.

**Every email must have** (CAN-SPAM): your real business name, a valid postal address in the signature, and a working way to opt out. Honor opt-outs within 10 business days.

Merge fields: `{first_name}`, `{practice}`, `{neighborhood}`, `{sender}`.

---

## 1. Medical practices

**Email 1, day 1**
Subject: After-hours cleaning for {practice}

> Hi {first_name},
>
> I run Clean by Morning, an evening cleaning company for medical offices in {neighborhood}. We clean after your last patient leaves: exam rooms and restrooms disinfected, trash out, floors done, and photos texted before you open.
>
> It's one fixed monthly price, month to month. Would it help if I sent a quote? I just need your rough square footage and how many nights a week.
>
> {sender}

**Email 2, day 4**
Subject: Re: After-hours cleaning for {practice}

> Hi {first_name}, one more detail in case it's useful: we use color-coded cloths so restroom cloths never touch exam rooms, and we never handle sharps or regulated waste. That stays with your hauler.
>
> A 2,000 sq ft practice cleaned three nights a week is about $975 a month with us. Happy to price yours after a 15-minute walkthrough.

**Email 3, day 9**
Subject: Close the loop?

> Hi {first_name}, I'll stop here. If your current cleaning ever falls short, here's a 1-minute price estimate: cleanbymorning.com/pricing/. Reply anytime and I'll come by for a walkthrough.

## Replies to expect

| They say | You reply |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have someone." | "Makes sense. If anything changes, I can quote in a day. Mind if I check back in three months?" |
| "How much?" | Ask for square footage and nights; reply with the estimator number the same day and offer a walkthrough to confirm. |
| "Send info." | Send the capability statement link and propose two walkthrough times. |
| "Not interested." | Thank them and remove them from the list. |
