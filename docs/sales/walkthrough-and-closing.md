# Walkthrough, proposal and close

The goal of every walkthrough: leave with the information for a fixed monthly price, and get a yes within 48 hours.

## Before you go

- [ ] Look up the practice on Google: square footage, number of providers, hours
- [ ] Bring a tape measure or laser measure, your phone (photos), and a printed capability statement
- [ ] Have the estimator open on your phone (cleanbymorning.com/pricing/)

## Walkthrough checklist (15 minutes)

- [ ] Square footage (measure or ask for the lease number)
- [ ] Rooms: exam rooms or operatories, offices, waiting room, kitchen or break room
- [ ] Restrooms: how many
- [ ] Floors: hard floor, carpet, rubber
- [ ] Nights a week and the earliest start time
- [ ] Access: key, fob, alarm code, security desk, building service hours
- [ ] Building rules: certificate of insurance required? Freight elevator? Trash location?
- [ ] Who supplies paper, soap and liners
- [ ] What's wrong with the current cleaner (write their words down; it's your pitch)
- [ ] Decision-maker and when they'll decide
- [ ] Photos of each area (for your own pricing and checklist)

## Proposal (send within 24 hours)

> **Subject:** Cleaning proposal for {practice}
>
> Hi {first_name},
>
> Thanks for walking me through the office. Here's what we'd do:
>
> **Schedule:** {nights} nights a week ({days}), starting after {time}
> **Every visit:** {paste the checklist for their space type}
> **Monthly price:** ${monthly} plus sales tax, billed on the 1st by ACH autopay
> **Supplies:** included; restroom paper and soap restocked at cost if you'd like
> **Terms:** month to month, 30 days' notice to cancel either way
> **Start date:** {date}, with the first month paid before the first clean
>
> To start, reply "yes" and I'll send the agreement and autopay link.
>
> {sender}

## Close

- Follow up 48 hours after the proposal if there's no reply: "Want me to hold {date} for your first night?"
- Offer two start dates, not an open question.
- If price is the objection, change the scope (fewer nights, or every other night for low-traffic rooms), not the hourly rate.

## Objections

| Objection | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| "Too expensive." | "We can drop to {fewer} nights a week, which brings it to ${lower}. Restrooms and trash can still be done nightly." |
| "We have a cleaner." | "What would you change about them?" Then solve exactly that. |
| "Are you insured?" | Only say yes once the policy is bound. Then: "Yes. I'll send the certificate today, naming your building if needed." |
| "You're new." | "That's why the owner cleans or inspects every visit, and it's month to month. If we're not right for you, you leave with 30 days' notice." |
| "We need a contract." | "Happy to sign yours, or use our one-page agreement: month to month, 30 days' notice." |
| "Can you start tomorrow?" | Say yes only if you can. A missed first night loses the account. |

## After they say yes: onboarding

- [ ] Signed service agreement (see `service-agreement-template.md`)
- [ ] First month collected by ACH or card
- [ ] Autopay set for the 1st of each month
- [ ] Certificate of insurance sent to the client and to building management, if required
- [ ] Keys or fob received and logged; alarm code stored securely (never in texts or email)
- [ ] Site checklist written for their space, with photos
- [ ] Start date and time confirmed; contact for problems at night
- [ ] First night: before-and-after photos texted when done
- [ ] After two weeks: ask how it's going; after a month: ask for a Google review
