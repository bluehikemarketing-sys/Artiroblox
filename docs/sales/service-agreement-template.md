# Cleaning service agreement (template)

> This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have a New York attorney review it once before you use it.

**Between:** Clean by Morning Office Cleaning ("Clean by Morning") and {Client legal name} ("Client"), for the premises at {address}.

**1. Services.** Clean by Morning will clean the premises {nights} nights a week ({days}), after {time}, following the checklist attached as Schedule A. Changes to the checklist are agreed in writing (email is fine).

**2. Price and payment.** Client pays ${monthly} per month plus applicable sales tax. The first month is due before the first clean. After that, Clean by Morning charges the account on file by ACH on the 1st of each month. Payments more than 10 days late incur a late fee of ${late_fee}, and service may be paused until the account is current. Supplies billed at cost (paper, soap, liners) are added to the next invoice.

**3. One-time work.** Deep cleans, post-construction and move-out cleans are quoted separately. A 50% deposit is due to book, and the balance is due on completion.

**4. Term and cancellation.** This agreement runs month to month. Either side may end it with 30 days' written notice. Prepaid amounts for days after the end date are refunded.

**5. Access and security.** Client provides keys, fobs and alarm codes. Clean by Morning stores them securely, arms the alarm on leaving, and returns all keys when the agreement ends. Clean by Morning does not open files, drawers or screens.

**6. Exclusions.** Clean by Morning does not handle sharps, regulated medical waste, hazardous materials or biohazards, and does not move heavy furniture or equipment.

**7. Quality.** If Client reports a missed item within 24 hours, Clean by Morning will fix it on the next visit or sooner at no charge.

**8. Insurance.** Clean by Morning carries general liability insurance of at least ${gl_limit} per occurrence and will provide a certificate of insurance on request. *(Only include this section once the policy is bound.)*

**9. Non-solicitation.** For the term of this agreement and 12 months after, Client will not directly hire a Clean by Morning cleaner assigned to the premises without written consent.

**10. Liability.** Clean by Morning is responsible for damage caused by its negligence. Client should report any damage within 48 hours.
