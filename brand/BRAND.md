# Clean by Morning brand kit

**Clean by Morning** (registered name: *Clean by Morning Office Cleaning*) is evening commercial cleaning for medical offices, dental practices, offices and gyms in Brooklyn, Manhattan and Nassau County.

**The promise:** you lock up at 6, and it's clean by morning.

## Voice

- Plain, calm and specific. Say what gets cleaned, when, and what it costs.
- Write for an office manager or practice owner who has been let down by cleaners before.
- Use: *walkthrough, checklist, monthly price, after you close, the same crew*.
- Avoid: *spotless, sparkling, maids, best in NYC*, and any claim you can't prove yet (insured, bonded, reviews) until it's true.

## Type

**Sentient** (Indian Type Foundry, via Fontshare), self-hosted from `site/static/fonts/`. Light (300) for headlines, Regular (400) for text, Medium (500) for buttons. One typeface everywhere.

## Logo

- **Wordmark:** "Clean by Morning" set in Sentient Regular. Use `wordmark-on-blue.png` where the background is dark or cobalt, and `wordmark-on-white.png` on white paper and documents.
- **Mark** (favicon and tiny sizes): a cobalt square with a yellow bar near the bottom, the line where night turns into morning. The build writes it as the site's `favicon.svg`.
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the "C" around the wordmark. Don't stretch it, outline it or add effects.

## Photos

Night and early-morning New York, shown as blue monotone (the photo blended into cobalt). Replace the stock city photos with your own as soon as you have them: finished offices at night (with the client's permission), your equipment, the street outside a client's building at dusk. Never show a client's patients, screens or files.
