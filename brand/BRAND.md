## Voice

Do:

- Say exactly what's included and what it costs
- Use plain, calm sentences
- Show proof: photos, checklists, policies
- Name real places: Pelican Bay, Marco Island, Estero

Don't:

- Claim results you can't show
- Use 'sparkling', 'spotless' or 'maids'
- Post reviews or before-and-after photos without the client's written permission
- Promise availability you don't have
