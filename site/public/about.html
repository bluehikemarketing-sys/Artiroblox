About

A Naples cleaning company built around proof

Many Naples homeowners aren't home when their house gets cleaned. They're at work, at the club, or up north for the summer. We built Sea Grape so they can still see that the job was done right.

Every clean follows a written, room-by-room checklist for that home. When the cleaner finishes, the client gets photos and the completed checklist by text. Payment happens after the clean, not before.

Our cleaners are background-checked before their first visit, and every clean is insured. If something isn't right, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back to fix it at no charge.

We're named after the sea grape, the round-leafed shrub that grows along Southwest Florida's beaches and holds the dunes in place.
