One night at your office

A typical evening at a 2,000 square foot practice, cleaned three nights a week.

  1. 6:00PM

    You lock up

    Leave when your last patient does. We already have the key, the alarm code and your checklist.

  2. 6:30PM

    We let ourselves in

    Usually the same person every night, through the entrance your building assigns. Alarm off, lights on.

  3. 7:00PM

    Exam rooms and operatories

    Counters, chairs, door handles and switches disinfected, with EPA-registered products left wet for their full contact time.

  4. 8:15PM

    Restrooms

    Scrubbed with their own color-coded cloths, disinfected, and restocked with paper and soap.

  5. 9:00PM

    Floors and trash

    Carpets vacuumed, hard floors damp-mopped with a clean head for each area, trash and recycling out.

  6. 9:45PM

    Lights off, alarm on

    We photograph every room, set the alarm and lock the door behind us.

  7. 3:00AM

    The office sits clean

    Nothing moves until morning.

  8. 6:30AM

    Photos in your inbox

    Pictures of every room, sent when we finished, waiting when you wake up.

  9. 7:45AM

    You open

    Your team walks into a clean office. Billing is one monthly autopay, with nothing to chase.

Questions

How much does office cleaning cost?

We charge by cleaning time: about $50 per hour, with a $75 minimum per visit. A 2,000 square foot medical office cleaned three nights a week is about $975 a month. The estimator shows your number.

When do you clean?

After you close, usually between 6 PM and 2 AM. You pick the nights, and we're done before you open.

Who does the cleaning?

Clean by Morning is owner-operated. The owner cleans or inspects every account, so you have one person responsible.

Do you clean medical and dental offices?

Yes, they're our focus. We use color-coded microfiber and EPA-registered disinfectants, and we never handle sharps or regulated medical waste.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by ACH autopay on the 1st. The first month is paid before the first clean. One-time jobs take a 50% deposit.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month to month. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice.

Email or text your square footage and how many nights a week. We reply with a price by the next morning.
