What we clean

Five kinds of space. Each gets its own written checklist.

  • Medical office cleaningExam rooms, waiting room and restrooms, disinfected every visit.
  • Dental office cleaningOperatory floors, sterilization area, reception and restrooms.
  • Office cleaningDesks, kitchens, restrooms and floors, before your team arrives.
  • Gym and studio cleaningStudio floors, locker rooms, showers and equipment touch points.
  • Post-construction cleaningA one-time clean after a build-out, so you can open.

How we work

Owner-operated, so one person is responsible for your office.

  • The owner cleans or inspects every account
  • A written checklist for your space, agreed at the walkthrough
  • Photos of every room after each clean, if you want them
  • Keys and alarm codes stored securely; the alarm set when we leave

Questions

When do you clean?

After you close, usually between 6 PM and 2 AM. You pick the nights, and we're done before you open.

Who does the cleaning?

Clean by Morning is owner-operated. The owner cleans or inspects every account, so you have one person responsible.

Do you clean medical and dental offices?

Yes, they're our focus. We use color-coded microfiber and EPA-registered disinfectants, and we never handle sharps or regulated medical waste.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by ACH autopay on the 1st. The first month is paid before the first clean. One-time jobs take a 50% deposit.

How is pricing set?

Every space is different, so we price after a free 15-minute walkthrough: the size, the number of rooms and restrooms, and how many nights a week. You get a fixed monthly price in writing within 24 hours.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month to month. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice.
