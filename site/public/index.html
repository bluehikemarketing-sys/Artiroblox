Questions, answered

All questions

How much does house cleaning cost in Naples?

Our standard clean starts at $110 for a 1-bedroom, 1-bathroom home, and a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home is $175. Recurring plans save up to 20%. The price tool shows your exact price before you book.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

No. Many clients are at work or out of town. Leave a key in a lockbox or give us a door or gate code, and we'll text you when we're done.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes, every time. If you prefer a product, leave it out and we'll use it.

What's your guarantee?

If something isn't right, tell us within 48 hours and we'll come back and re-clean it free. If it's still not right after that, we refund the clean.

How do I pay?

You pay after the clean is finished, never before.

Can I get the same cleaner every time?

Yes. Ask for the same cleaner and we'll match you whenever possible.

What's the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

A deep clean adds detail work a standard clean skips: baseboards, door frames, cabinet fronts, grout and hard-water spots.

Do you clean Airbnbs and vacation rentals?

Yes. We do turnovers between guests and photograph anything broken, missing or running low.
