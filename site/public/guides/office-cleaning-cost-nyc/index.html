How much does office cleaning cost in NYC?

Most commercial cleaning in New York is priced by the hours it takes, multiplied by how many nights a week you need it. Here's how that works, with our own prices as examples.

Book a walkthroughSee prices

What sets the price

  • Square footage: a routine office cleans at roughly 2,000–3,000 square feet an hour; medical and dental spaces take longer because every room is disinfected.
  • Nights a week: most offices book two to five; gyms often book five to seven.
  • Restrooms: each one adds time every visit.
  • Building rules: Manhattan buildings often limit service hours and require a certificate of insurance, which can affect scheduling.

Worked examples (Clean by Morning prices)

  • 1,000 sq ft office, 3 nights a week: about $975 a month
  • 2,000 sq ft medical office, 3 nights a week: about $975 a month
  • 3,500 sq ft dental office, 5 nights a week: about $2,380 a month
  • 5,000 sq ft office, 3 nights a week: about $1,690 a month

Sales tax

  • Commercial cleaning is generally subject to New York sales tax. The rate depends on where the work is done: 8.875% in New York City and 8.625% in Nassau County. Ask any cleaner whether their quote includes it.

Questions to ask any cleaning company

  • Is the price fixed monthly, or does it change with hours?
  • Is there a contract term, and how much notice do I need to cancel?
  • Can you send a certificate of insurance before you start?
  • Who will clean my office, and will it be the same person each night?
