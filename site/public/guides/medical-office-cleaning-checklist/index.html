Medical office cleaning checklist

A written checklist is what makes a medical office clean the same way every night. This is the list we work from, which you can adapt for your own practice.

Book a walkthroughSee prices

Every night

  • Exam rooms: counters, chairs, exam table surfaces outside the paper, door handles and light switches disinfected
  • Waiting room: chairs, front desk, pens, clipboards and door handles
  • Restrooms: toilets, sinks, mirrors, dispensers; paper and soap restocked
  • Trash and recycling emptied; liners replaced
  • Hard floors damp-mopped, carpets vacuumed

Every week

  • Baseboards and vents dusted
  • Inside microwaves and break-room fridge fronts
  • Chair legs and bases wiped
  • Glass partitions and entry doors

Every month

  • High dusting: light fixtures, tops of cabinets
  • Upholstered waiting-room chairs vacuumed
  • Floor machine scrub or carpet spot treatment as needed

How it should be done

  • Color-coded microfiber, so restroom cloths never touch exam rooms
  • EPA-registered disinfectants, left wet for the contact time on the label
  • Work from clean areas to dirty areas, and high surfaces to low

What a cleaner should never handle

  • Sharps containers and regulated medical waste: these go with your licensed medical waste hauler
  • Patient files, screens and anything in drawers
