How often should a dental office be cleaned?

Most dental practices book cleaning three to five nights a week. The right number depends on patient volume and how many operatories you run.

Book a walkthroughSee prices

A rule of thumb

  • Open four or five days with full schedules: clean every night you're open
  • Open three days or fewer: clean the nights after you see patients
  • Restrooms and reception: every visit, whatever the schedule

What the cleaning crew does

  • Operatory floors, counters outside the clinical zone, door handles and switches
  • Sterilization-area floors and sinks
  • Reception, restrooms, break room and trash

What stays with your clinical team

  • Chairside surfaces and equipment between patients
  • Sharps, amalgam and regulated waste, through your licensed hauler

Pricing it

  • A 2,000 sq ft practice cleaned four nights a week is about $1,300 a month at our rates.
  • The estimator on our pricing page shows other sizes and schedules.
