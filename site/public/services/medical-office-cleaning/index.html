Medical office cleaning in NYC

Evening cleaning for medical practices in Brooklyn, Manhattan and Nassau County, done to a written checklist and finished before your first patient.

Book a walkthroughQuestions

Every visit

The checklist we start from. We adjust it to your space at the walkthrough.

  • Exam rooms: counters, chairs, door handles and light switches disinfected
  • Waiting room: seats, front desk and touch points wiped; floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Restrooms scrubbed and disinfected; paper and soap restocked
  • Trash and recycling out to your building's area
  • Hard floors damp-mopped with a clean head for every area
  • Break room: counters, sink, microwave and fridge front

How we work

  • Color-coded microfiber: restroom cloths never touch exam rooms
  • EPA-registered disinfectants, left wet for the label's contact time
  • We never handle sharps containers or regulated medical waste; those stay with your licensed hauler
  • We don't open files, drawers or screens. Patient information stays where you left it

Questions about medical office cleaning

Do you clean exam rooms every night?

Yes. Every exam room on the schedule gets its surfaces and touch points disinfected each visit.

Do you handle medical waste?

No. Sharps containers and regulated medical waste are handled by your licensed medical waste hauler. We empty regular trash and recycling only.

Which disinfectants do you use?

EPA-registered hospital disinfectants, applied and left wet for the contact time on the label. If your practice has a required product, we'll use it.
