Every visit

  • Exam rooms: counters, chairs, door handles and light switches disinfected

Questions about medical office cleaning

Do you clean exam rooms every night?

Yes. Every exam room on the schedule gets its surfaces and touch points disinfected each visit.

Do you handle medical waste?

No. Sharps containers and regulated medical waste are handled by your licensed medical waste hauler. We empty regular trash and recycling only.

Which disinfectants do you use?

EPA-registered hospital disinfectants, applied and left wet for the contact time on the label. If your practice has a required product, we'll use it.
