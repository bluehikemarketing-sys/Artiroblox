Questions about move-in and move-out cleaning

Does the home need to be empty?

Yes, or nearly. Furniture left in place limits what we can reach, especially floors and closets.

Will this get my deposit back?

We clean to the standard most Naples landlords inspect for, and send photos you can share. The deposit decision is your landlord's.

Do you work with realtors and property managers?

Yes. We work from lockboxes, schedule around showings and closings, and send photos for your file.

Do you clean before a move-in too?

Yes. The same service works for a home you're moving into.
