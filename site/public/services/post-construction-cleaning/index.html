Post-construction cleaning in NYC

A one-time clean after a build-out or renovation, so a new office or practice can open: dust, debris, windows, fixtures and floors.

Book a walkthroughQuestions

What we clean

The checklist we start from. We adjust it to your space at the walkthrough.

  • Construction dust removed from every surface, ledge and vent
  • Interior windows, frames and sills
  • Fixtures, cabinets and drawers inside and out
  • Floors vacuumed, scrubbed and mopped
  • Stickers, paint spots and adhesive removed where safe
  • Final wipe-down so the space is move-in ready

How we work

  • Quoted after a walkthrough of the finished space
  • Deposit to book, the rest when the space passes your walkthrough
  • We coordinate with your contractor's punch list
  • Medical and dental build-outs cleaned to opening standard

Questions about post-construction cleaning

When should post-construction cleaning happen?

After trades finish and before furniture or equipment moves in. A second light clean after move-in is often worth it.

How is it priced?

By the size of the space and how much construction dust is left. We quote after a walkthrough.

Do you remove construction debris?

We remove dust and small debris. Large debris and dumpsters stay with your contractor.
