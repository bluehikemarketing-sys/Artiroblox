Questions about deep cleaning

What's the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

A deep clean adds the detail work: baseboards, door frames, cabinet fronts, grout, hard-water spots and stovetop grates. It takes about one and a half times as long.

How long does a deep clean take?

About 5 hours for a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home with one cleaner, or less with two.

Is the inside of the oven included?

It's an add-on, priced upfront in the price tool, because ovens vary a lot.

Do I need to move things before you arrive?

Clear counters and floors where you can. We don't move heavy furniture.
