Questions about standard cleaning

How long does a standard clean take?

About 3 hours for a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home with one cleaner. Larger homes get two cleaners.

Is standard cleaning enough for a first visit?

If the home was cleaned professionally in the last month, yes. Otherwise we recommend a deep clean first so later visits hold that level.
