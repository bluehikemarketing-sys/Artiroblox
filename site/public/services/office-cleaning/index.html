Office cleaning in NYC

Evening office cleaning for small and mid-size offices: desks, kitchens, restrooms and floors, ready before your team arrives.

Book a walkthroughQuestions

Every visit

The checklist we start from. We adjust it to your space at the walkthrough.

  • Desks and work surfaces wiped where clear
  • Kitchen: counters, sink, microwave, appliance fronts
  • Restrooms scrubbed and restocked
  • Trash and recycling out
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Doors, handles, switches and glass smudges

How we work

  • Same cleaner and checklist each night whenever possible
  • Keys and alarm codes stored securely; we arm the alarm when we leave
  • Photos texted after the clean, if you want them
  • Supplies included; restroom paper and soap restocked at cost

Questions about office cleaning

Do you clean desks?

We wipe clear desk surfaces. We don't move papers or touch computers.

How often should an office be cleaned?

Most offices book two to five nights a week, depending on headcount and kitchen use.

Can you clean during the day?

We focus on evenings so your team isn't disturbed. Ask about day porter work.
