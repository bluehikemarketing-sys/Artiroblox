Cleaning in Manhattan

Most Manhattan office buildings require vendors to send a certificate of insurance naming building management before working after hours. We send it before your first night.

Many medical practices on the Upper East Side and in Murray Hill share buildings with other offices, so the clean has to fit the building's service hours. We plan the route around them.

We clean small and mid-size spaces: single-floor practices, studios and offices up to about 15,000 square feet.

In Manhattan we clean in the Financial District, Tribeca, the Flatiron District, Midtown, Murray Hill, the Upper East Side and the Upper West Side.
