Cleaning in Nassau County

Nassau has a large share of medical and dental practices in office parks and medical buildings with their own parking, which makes evening cleaning straightforward.

Practices in Garden City, Mineola, Great Neck and Lake Success often run late appointments. We schedule after the last patient and finish before the morning team.

We're a short drive from the city line, so Nassau accounts get the same checklist and the same nightly photos as our NYC clients.

In Nassau County we clean in Garden City, Mineola, Great Neck, Manhasset, Lake Success, Hicksville, Rockville Centre and Massapequa.
