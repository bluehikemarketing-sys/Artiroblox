A cleaner way to book

SunnyMop is a new Naples house cleaning company built to be easy to trust.

Hiring a cleaner shouldn't feel like a gamble. So we show every price upfront, we charge only after the clean, and if something's missed we come back free within 48 hours.

Every visit follows a written, room-by-room checklist, so the clean you get on your tenth visit matches the first. You can ask for the same cleaner every time, and if you're away, we text finish photos.

We clean homes, condos and vacation rentals across Naples, from Old Naples to East Naples.
