One crew, one checklist

Clean by Morning is an owner-operated cleaning company for medical practices and small offices in New York City and Nassau County.

Book a walkthrough

Why we work this way

The usual complaints about commercial cleaners are a different person every month, a checklist nobody follows, and an invoice that keeps changing. Clean by Morning is set up to avoid all three.

The owner cleans or inspects every account. Every visit follows the same written checklist. Prices are fixed and billed once a month, and you can leave with 30 days' notice.

We clean in the evening, after you close, so your patients and staff never see us, only the result.

What you can count on

  • A written checklist for your space, agreed at the walkthrough
  • Photos of every room after each clean, if you want them
  • Keys and alarm codes stored securely; the alarm set when we leave
  • One fixed monthly price, month to month
