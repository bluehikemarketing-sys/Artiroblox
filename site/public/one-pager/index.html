Capability statement

Clean by Morning Office Cleaning: evening commercial cleaning for medical offices, dental practices, offices and gyms in Brooklyn, Manhattan and Nassau County.

Services

  • Medical office cleaning
  • Dental office cleaning
  • Office cleaning
  • Gym and studio cleaning
  • Post-construction cleaning

How we work

  • Owner-operated; the owner cleans or inspects every account
  • Written checklist per site; photos after each visit on request
  • Color-coded microfiber and EPA-registered disinfectants
  • No handling of sharps or regulated medical waste
  • Keys and alarm codes stored securely

Commercial terms

  • Fixed monthly price, ACH autopay on the 1st
  • Month to month, 30 days' notice
  • From $75 per visit; about $50 per cleaning hour
  • NAICS 561720, Janitorial Services
  • Certificate of insurance sent on request once coverage is in place
